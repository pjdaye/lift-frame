Reason codes turn subjective failures into operational signals.

## Common codes

- RIGHTS_VIOLATION (S1)
- EXTERNAL_URL_PRESENT (S1)
- CITATION_MISSING (S2)
- CITATION_INVALID_DOCID (S2)
- RETRY_FAILED (S2)
- DRIFT_DETECTED_SCORE_DROP (S3)
- VARIANCE_SPIKE (S3)
